MOTION MEASUREMENT
- Edge rendering stayed near 120 FPS (about 8.35 ms per frame).
- 60 Hz snapshots averaged about 16.2–16.4 ms, but local timer catch-up produced
  occasional back-to-back delivery and gaps of roughly 26–28 ms.
- Cause: v0.1.29 based two-frame interpolation on a moving average of packet
  arrival intervals. Late packets exhausted that interval and briefly paused;
  the next frame then restarted against a changed timing estimate. Boost made
  the same positional discontinuity more visible because screen travel doubled.
- v0.1.30–v0.1.35 kept movement physics unchanged and rendered 50 ms behind the newest
  authoritative simulation time, using a median arrival-to-simulation clock
  estimate. This absorbs measured jitter without extrapolation or rollback.
- The small non-ping delay reported in v0.1.35 was that deliberate 50 ms render
  buffer. v0.1.36 reduces it to 34 ms (about two 60 Hz snapshots), removing
  roughly one frame of added delay while retaining timestamp interpolation.
- Boost/brake testing confirms no energy drain while braking, continued facing
  control, continued boost glow, and unchanged movement physics values.
- The minimap and event overlay added no sustained FPS loss in Edge testing.
